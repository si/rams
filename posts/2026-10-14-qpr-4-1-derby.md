## The People, Not Just the Sheet

### Sondre Langås
Gone after roughly ten minutes, hurt blocking Rayan Kolli's effort after Paul Smyth's good work. Eustace says he's "in a bad way" and heading for a scan. With the defence already threadbare, losing your best centre-back that early was never going to end well, and it didn't. Fingers crossed for the scan.

### Ryan Nyambe
Dragged into the middle of the back four when Langås went off, because that's where the injury list has left us. Not his natural job, and the evening showed it, but he was only there because there was nobody else. [SI - add anything specific you saw]

### Josh Vickers
Derbyshire Live gave him a 4. A couple of decent stops kept it from being worse, but four goals is four goals. Hard to pin much on him behind that defence.

### Joe Ward
Rated a 3 by Derbyshire Live, who weren't alone in finding him and others wanting in a poor first half. [SI - your view; I've kept it light because I've not seen the game]

### Mohammed Fuseini
Beaten on the left by Boy Kemper, who back-heeled the ball to Alfie Lloyd for QPR's opener on 18 minutes. Not all on him, but it's the kind of moment that sets a night's tone. [SI - add anything on his attacking play]

### Lars-Jørgen Salvesen
The one positive: a goal in the 90th minute-plus-lots, taking him to two for the season. It was a consolation in the purest sense, but it was a goal, and he's now our impact sub of choice. Not that it mattered.

### The manager
Eustace admitted the first goal was conceded poorly and says he's "confident we'll come through it". Seven games without a win and a growing injury list make that a brave line. [SI - add the rest of his post-match comments if you've got them]

## The Read

Four goals conceded, three of them before half-time, with the first player down hurt inside a quarter of an hour. That's the story, and it's mostly a squad-depth one.

Derby are now winless in six, second from bottom according to the post-match reports, and the injury list is long enough that Nyambe is playing centre-back. Eustace has said the right things, but there's only so long you can talk about "difficult moments".

QPR looked like a team with a plan and fit players. We looked like neither. [SI - your own take on selection/shape, and whether Eustace's job is under pressure]
