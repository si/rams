## What changed after publishing (lessons folded into the skill)

- **No images made it in.** The Canva MCP path kept failing on save-to-account
  approval; the design itself generated fine. Fix used: download the design
  directly from its Canva web link and upload manually to Patreon, rather
  than relying on the automation to push it through.
- **Emoji mostly didn't survive the paste** into Patreon's editor. Fix: type
  the few that matter (⚽, 🔄) directly in Patreon rather than pasting them
  in from drafted text.
- **"IN THEIR WORDS" shipped as a dead placeholder** — the channels were
  quiet all pre-season (expected: SuperRams' own content plan scoped every
  pre-season friendly as a private pipeline test, with the real public
  launch at the Carabao Cup opener). Fix folded into the skill: either cut
  the section when there's nothing to pull, or replace it with a specific
  comment-seeding question tied to the match, rather than leaving a gap that
  reads as unfinished.
- **Opponent detail was deliberately minimal** — no Rotherham lineup, no
  player-by-player treatment of their side. This was a direct ask partway
  through drafting and is now baked into the skill as the default, not an
  exception: SuperRams' USP is Derby's players and fans, not the opposition.
