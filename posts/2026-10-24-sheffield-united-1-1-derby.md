## The Read

Derby are 23rd, with one win in twelve games. A point at Bramall Lane with ten men doesn't change that.

But it matters that Eustace said the lads are "very honest" and "whoever plays out there gives their best". Going down to ten and not folding is the sort of thing a bottom-three side needs to be able to do.

The trouble is the cost. Sanderson's red means a suspension and Meister's injury is an unknown, and both land before Millwall away.
