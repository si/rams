## The People, Not Just the Sheet

### Lewis Travis
Back in the side for the first time since August, handed the armband, and gone before the hour. Great to see him out there, rotten to see him limp off again. Derby's injury list is now less a list and more a lifestyle.

### Rhian Brewster
On at 52' for the injured Travis and almost immediately the most dangerous thing Derby had. His effort at 63' came back off the frame of the goal (some reports have it just wide, so treat the post as "reported"). Either way it was the closest we came to a point, and a reminder that there is real threat in there.

### David Ozoh
Booked at 76', then left foot, centre of the box, late on, and Viktor Johansson had to be at his very best to keep it out. Another one that deserved better. Also, Stoke's keeper is apparently allowed to have a good day against us for once.

### Ryan Nyambe
Dropped into the middle of the back line alongside Sanderson with Langås out for a long spell after surgery, and Hedges and Ward either side of them. Derby were beaten by a set piece rather than anything from open play, so the makeshift defence did more right than wrong.

### Mohammed Fuseini
Started on the left of the three behind Morris and gave way for Meister at 73'. Loan signings who earn a start and look part of it are a welcome change from the Burnley cameo-to-hero story.

### Josh Vickers
Beaten by a header that took a deflection on its way past him. Not one to hang on the keeper. Nothing else to reproach.

## The Read

Eustace said the lads gave him the reaction he wanted after QPR, and for once the eye test agrees with him.

Six or seven first-teamers missing, a makeshift back line, the captain going off hurt again, and Derby still hit the woodwork, forced a top save and made Stoke work for it.

That is a decent afternoon's work that finished with nothing. We know how that feels.

The honest bit: one win in eleven is one win in eleven, and being unlucky doesn't earn points. Set pieces have now cost us again.

The positive bit: this is the first time in a while that a loss has felt like a team that is still fighting rather than one that has stopped.

The fanbase is split on what happens next, as the quotes below show. Fair enough, there is still no home win this season. But the one thing nobody can say about Saturday is that the players downed tools.
