# Pre-match socials: Derby County v Birmingham City

- **Status:** Matchday social copy, drafted ahead of kick-off for Si to review
  and post manually (Bluesky/X/Facebook aren't connected to Buffer — see
  `metrics/README.md`). This is **not** the `patreon-match-post` write-up —
  per that skill, the full report gets drafted the day after (13 September),
  once the score, lineups, subs and stats are confirmed. This file is just
  the matchday hype posts plus the head-start facts for tomorrow's draft.
- **Fixture:** EFL Championship, Matchweek 7
- **Venue:** Pride Park Stadium, Derby
- **Date/kick-off:** Saturday 12 September 2026, KO 12:30 (11:30 UTC)

## Confirmed

- Derby have lost 3 of their first 4 home league games this season, most
  recently failing to break down 10-man West Brom in a midweek defeat.
- Birmingham arrive off their first loss of the season, away at Norwich,
  which ended their unbeaten start to the campaign.
- Table position: Birmingham well clear of Derby (sources put them ~11th-15th
  on 7 points; Derby down around 22nd on 4 points from 5 games — exact
  places conflicted slightly across sources, worth a quick check before
  quoting a number in the day-after post).
- Head-to-head: 110 meetings all-time, Derby 38 wins to Birmingham's 37.
  Last two meetings: 1-0 Derby (March), 1-1 draw (December).

## Team news

**Derby (out):** Carlton Morris (muscle injury, picked up this week),
Rhian Brewster, Craig Forsyth, Derry Murkin, Matt Clarke, Patrick Agyemang.

**Birmingham:** Patrick Roberts a doubt (knock); Demarai Gray doubtful after
missing the Norwich game.
