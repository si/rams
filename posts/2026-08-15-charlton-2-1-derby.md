THE READ

Three competitive-adjacent outings into the season and the story hasn't
changed: Derby can score, Derby cannot hold a lead. A 1-1 draw papered over
by pre-season not mattering, a 1-2 cup exit to League One opposition, and
now a lead thrown away against a newly promoted-feeling Charlton side on the
