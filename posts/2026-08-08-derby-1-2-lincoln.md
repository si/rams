THE PEOPLE, NOT JUST THE SHEET

Sam Szmodics
Three minutes gone and we're already chasing the game, so of course it's Szmodics who drags us back into it, chesting down a Taylor knockdown and burying a half-volley from 30 yards that's an early goal-of-the-season contender. Then Eustace hooks him at 79' anyway. If he's finding that level of quality in a cup tie we're losing, imagine what he'll do somewhere it stays 11 v 11 for the full 90.

Bobby Clark
The mazy run that teed up Morris was the best bit of individual play from anyone in a Derby shirt all afternoon, cup exit included. Just signed permanently in the summer, and already the one bit of the afternoon worth getting excited about.

Carlton Morris
Did his job. Cut inside, beat the keeper, and only a last-ditch Elerewe block stopped it going in. Give him service like Clark's all season and he'll have more of those; shame this one didn't count for anything.

Matt Clarke
Off at half-time with what Eustace confirmed afterwards is a recurrence of an issue he'd already picked up before kick-off — by his own account they "thought he'd be okay" and played him anyway. Bold call to make in a dead cup tie, and now there's an injury worry heading into Charlton that didn't need to exist.

Charlie Taylor
Hooked at 63'. Four days after being hooked at half-time against Rotherham. Two competitive minutes in and Eustace still doesn't fancy him lasting 90.

THE READ

No dressing this one up as a fair fight gone wrong — Lincoln are a League One side, a full division below us, and this was their first-ever win at Pride Park to prove it. Conceding twice inside the first 25 minutes to lower-league opposition is the real story here, cup exit or not. Szmodics gives you something to build on; the back four needs sorting fast, because Charlton next week is Championship opposition, and they won't be as forgiving as whatever let a League One side walk into Pride Park and do that.


WHAT'S NEXT

Cup's done for the year. **Saturday 15 August, The Valley: Charlton Athletic (a), Championship curtain-raiser.** First real points on the board, one way or the other.
