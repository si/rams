The story, not just the score

John Eustace tore his squad in two for this one — half at Mansfield on Friday night, the rest sent to Burton less than 24 hours later. It wasn't really a team sheet. It was a question: who's ready to be a Ram? Six Academy graduates started to answer it, and three of them put their names on the scoresheet.

Dajaune Brown broke the deadlock on 20 minutes with a finish that looked a lot older than his years. Mansfield's Michael Smith pulled the Stags level before the break — and came within a crossbar's width of a second, his header from a corner cannoning back off the woodwork early in the second half. It didn't matter. Charlie Smith and Cruz Allen both scored after the restart to settle it, either side of the experienced heads — Norwegian duo Sondre Langås and Lars-Jørgen Salvesen — making way for Osayande and Blackett-Taylor on the hour.

New First Team Coach Ben Wilkinson gave his reaction to RamsTV pitchside afterwards — well worth a watch for what the kids showed him. Catch it on dcfc.co.uk →
